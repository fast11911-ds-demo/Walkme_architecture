Interview Talking Point: Middleware Connector Synergy

"In this refined architecture, data flows sequentially from the presentation edge to the intelligence core. WalkMe captures front-end interactions and feeds them into SAP BTP (Cloud Integration). To showcase extreme enterprise extensibility, the pipeline utilizes established third-party integration connectors—such as MuleSoft Anypoint, Workato Recipes, or Boomi AtomSphere—to securely bridge and deliver the clean telemetry payloads straight into SAP Joule. Joule then leverages its internal Skill Templates to perform real-time cognitive reasoning and auto-trigger background executions."
